Словари слов (en.txt, ru.txt) / Word frequency lists (en.txt, ru.txt)

Списки основаны на проекте FrequencyWords (Hermit Dave), https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords,
построенном на корпусе субтитров OpenSubtitles (P. Lison, J. Tiedemann, "OpenSubtitles2016: Extracting Large Parallel Corpora from Movie and TV Subtitles", LREC 2016;
P. Lison, J. Tiedemann, M. Kouylekov, "OpenSubtitles2018: Statistical Rescoring of Sentence Alignments in Large, Noisy Parallel Corpora", LREC 2018; https://opus.nlpl.eu/).

Изменения: слова приведены к нижнему регистру, списки используются как частотный словарь для определения раскладки.

The lists are based on FrequencyWords by Hermit Dave, https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords, built from the OpenSubtitles corpus
(Lison and Tiedemann, LREC 2016; Lison, Tiedemann and Kouylekov, LREC 2018; https://opus.nlpl.eu/).
The list content is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
The files en.txt and ru.txt included in the app remain under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license.
Changes: words are lowercased; the lists are used as a frequency dictionary to detect the keyboard layout.
